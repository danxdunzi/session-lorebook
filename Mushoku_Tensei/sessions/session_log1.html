[LOG: Day 1; Night | Northern Continent, forest campfire] Campfire Reunion — Destinations Declared

  • Flow: Eris praised Rudeus — chantless magic, stone cannons, tidal waves, Grandpa's guards couldn't touch him — while Ryusei needled her enthusiasm | Ghislaine corroborated: "The boy's mana is vast. He defeated water spirits with bare intent" | Ten winters since the three last met | Ghislaine is not returning to Gal to serve — she escorts Eris to the Holy Land of Swords, the limit of her teaching | Eris vowed Sword King, no — Sword Emperor: "If I stay how I am, I'm useless! I couldn't protect Rudeus when it mattered!" — Gal to break her until she can cut a Dragon God's scales | Ghislaine: Gal Farion tolerates no softness; weak disciples die in the sanctum snow | Ryusei: escaped Gal alive, a decade-wandering rogue here to intercept a Water God | Eris begged to watch — "you'll just be in my way."
  • Impact: Ryusei = rogue exile, ten years gone from Gal's Sanctum; Water God intercept declared; Eris's drive rooted in failing Rudeus.

[LOG: Day 1; Night | Forest campfire] Past Lovers Exposed — Ghislaine's Heat History

  • Flow: Mate question posed bluntly; Eris scandalized — a "shameless barbarian" | Ghislaine: zero mates since Ryusei; central-territory males too fragile for a Dedoldia — one strike breaks them: no territory, no strong blood; "such seed is worthless" | Ryusei needled: "touched after me but didn't take them entirely"; her rebuttal — touching without taking is nothing; a beast knows a mate by scent, blood, and grip | History: the losing duelist alone kept coming back; near-daily hayloft trysts until they skipped sword practice — he alone endured her full fever and still gripped a hilt at daybreak | Eris shattered — blade leveled between his eyes, shrieking about sunrise discipline | Paul: no mate — an undisciplined stray of the Fang of the Black Wolf, pre-North; rutted through camp heats with whatever stood on two legs — Ghislaine, Elinalise, tavern girls; "the mud before the steel was forged."
  • Impact: Past intimacy confirmed — Ryusei unique among males, Paul discarded; Eris's image of her master's ascetic discipline broken.

[LOG: Day 1; Late night | Forest campfire] Regret Confrontation & Spar Agreement

  • Flow: Ryusei, staring into the fire: "If I hadn't run away..." — "the other I can't say yet today" | Ghislaine: he fled before Gal's blade cleaved him shoulder to hip; a stray who runs has no right to ask what the den would look like ten winters later | Eris: "Regret is for cowards!" — echoing her own leaving of Rudeus | Ghislaine agreed: old warmth only softens the neck for the Water God's counter | Ryusei: incomplete training doesn't mean he stopped growing — "you might have the title now, but I hope you don't look down on me"; Ghislaine: "a title is only leather and ink until steel meets bone" | Eris demanded an immediate duel; Ryusei declined, offering a first-light spar instead — his Water God warm-up — mocking "red girl" | Eris set first light, named herself Eris Boreas Greyrat, "you rogue bastard!" | Ghislaine stood the pup down and took first watch.
  • Impact: Spar set for Day 2 dawn; withheld statement left open; "red girl"/"rogue bastard" address dynamic locked.

[LOG: Day 2; Dawn | Forest clearing, frost and fog] Spar vs Eris — Karambit and the Vanishing Step

  • Flow: Eris in a low Superd-taught entry stance — trimmed for the Longsword of Light's first stride — that felled monsters across the Demon Continent; Julian masterwork drawn | Ryusei revealed a ring-locked karambit — "ever seen one, your majesty?" — "a curved tooth?!" | Ghislaine warned: the ring locks iron to bone — no wrist-strike disarm; the talon curve hooks under armor to tear sinew | Eris charged anyway — "too high," ribs exposed — diagonal executioner slash | Ryusei vanished mid-swing — no mana flare, pure footwork, instantaneous displacement — reappearing across the clearing at Ghislaine's chin; if pure footwork, his ankles should have shattered | Her second charge sidestepped; a neck cut she never felt — "You already lost, red girl... try to grasp my masterpiece" | Ghislaine: dead the instant her lead foot moved — flesh parted without dragging the edge; assassination precision | Clay-phial ointment — scarless by midday; "beautiful skin" flattery | Eris rejected the loss — "you ran away like a coward before my second swing!"
  • Impact: Tier gap exposed — displacement step, kill-precision, Hiramune never drawn; Eris never sensed the blade; Ghislaine: that timing will trouble the Water God — and the pup needed to taste true speed before Gal's dawn.

[LOG: Day 2; Morning | Forest clearing] Confession and the Den Pact

  • Flow: Ghislaine's farewell: go find your Water God — "a Dedoldia does not forget a mate who took the den" | Ryusei: "If you still have time to settle down... I still do like you" | Eris shrieked "steal my sword master away?!" until Ghislaine's tail cinched her waist mid-rant | Ghislaine, unmoved: "I have not forgotten the heat in the hayloft either" — a male staking a true claim | But her blade is Eris's until the girl reaches the pinnacle of the Sword God | Pact, fist extended: survive the Water God; when Eris takes her title and hunts alone, follow her trail — "if your lungs still draw air, we will share the den" | Eris hauled her master uphill by the harness strap, ears burning — Emperor twice as fast now | "Till we meet again" — Ghislaine never glanced back; two paths forged in steel.
  • Impact: Den pact standing (post-title reunion); Eris mortified, goal escalated; parties split — Eris/Ghislaine toward the Sword Sanctum, Ryusei toward his intercept.

[LOG: Day 2; Mid-morning | Northern mountain pass] Ambush of the Water God Party

  • Flow: Ryusei stepped from the brush posing as lost — footfall silent on the frost | Isolte's rapier cleared two inches: three paces, name and purpose | Reida, kiseru trailing smoke, read him instantly — clean leather, predator's gravity, curved iron in his pockets; "a clumsy lie for a boy who hid in the brush until the wind blew downstream" | Ryusei: merely traveling — he heard the Water God has a beautiful granddaughter | Isolte, Asuran knight, drew fully: "I am Isolte Cruel, Water King and premier disciple of the Water God Style! The living legend is Lady Reida Reida herself!" | Reida — lost wolf / young wolf — "Ask for her hand in marriage, or draw whatever steel you've got tucked in those pockets?" | He crossed the three-pace line; Isolte's mana-reinforced lunge — "Yield!" — disarmed off her own thrust, Flow turned on its heir; he knelt, holding her hand: "May I take your hand for marriage?" | Reida howled — steel stripped with the Flow, spun like a lady at a royal ball, proposal in the same breath; Isolte has husband-hunted three seasons: "a capable suitor!"
  • Impact: Rapier disarmed first exchange; proposal lodged; Isolte crimson; the wolf read, the trap unseen.

[LOG: Day 2; Mid-morning | Mountain pass] Reida's Foot Severed by Delayed Strike

  • Flow: Still kneeling, Ryusei looked at Reida's footing: "I didn't know... you only have one foot, Lady Reida" — as her severed ankle and silk boot thudded into the snow | The delayed cut was dealt from the bush before he spoke — no swing, no intent; nerves registered their death only when bone bore weight; her stance broken before the first word | Reida slammed against the boulder, kiseru dropped | Isolte tore free, knelt, sealed the stump with Healing, threw her armored body over her master — "The Royal Knights of Asura will hunt you across every continent!" | Ryusei let the healing finish unimpeded | Reida, saber undrawn: he came to butcher a God, not for ransoms — "draw your little curved tooth and finish the kill, or keep talking to a cripple" | Ryusei taunted: born earlier, he'd "have a taste of a more primed fruit" | Reida: "You would have choked on the branch forty years ago, little wolf."
  • Impact: Left ankle gone — foundation destroyed; Isolte's mana drained; Asura-hunt seeded; Reida perceives the gulf.

[LOG: Day 2; Mid-morning | Mountain pass, before Reida] Assault on Isolte

  • Flow: "I'll let you see a scene of what I could've been, Reida" — he took her disciple from behind, forced down on all fours in the bloodied snow before her immobilized master | Taunted: "A Water King on all fours, serving a mere rogue — is this the disciple of the Water God?"; dared her to redirect him with the Water Style — her school's grace found no leverage | Slapped her rear — "Isn't this the same way Isolte would've been made if it weren't for you too, Reida?" | Lifted her upright, legs pinned to her torso at Reida's eye level — "Let's give her a proper view, my love" | Isolte begged her master not to look, apologizing as her body betrayed her — climaxing unwilling as he finished inside her, release spilling onto Reida's blood-soaked silks: "I hate you... I hate you...!" | Reida, vision graying: "A coward who hides behind a maiden's tears... may the gods pity your soul, boy" | Isolte went limp, then fainted; Reida faded toward death.
  • Impact: Isolte violated before her dying master; carried off unconscious; "my love" prefigures the wife framing.
