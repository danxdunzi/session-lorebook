Shierra

Keys: Shierra

SHORT_DESC: {Skilled magician, scroll artisan, and accountant of Paul Greyrat's Fittoa Search Group and Begaritt Rescue Party [Birth: K400]; younger adventuring partner to warrior Vierra; a shy, timid, and petite woman who hides behind her long bangs and her sister's shadow; proficient in water, earth, and defensive incantation magic, as well as rapid magic scroll transcription, having previously worked copying scrolls for the Millis Church; joined Paul's Search Party in Millishion following the Fittoa Incident, managing party finances, medical supplies, and magical artillery; accompanied the group across the sea to Begaritt, where she copied fifty complex teleportation and defensive scrolls in a single day for the Teleport Labyrinth raid; wept bitterly upon Paul's tragic death, generously donating her expedition share to Lillia and Rudeus; traveled with the survivors back to Ranoa and Asura, where she retired peacefully with Vierra | [Personality & Quirks] Extremely shy, introverted, and easily flustered, instinctively shrinking behind Vierra or pulling her hood low over her eyes in the presence of strangers or aggressive men; possessed astonishing speed and dexterity with ink and quill, capable of accurately drafting fifty high-grade magic scrolls in a single day using Rudeus's references; served as Paul's diligent party accountant, fretting constantly over supply budgets and healing potions; deeply compassionate beneath her timid exterior—burst into agonized tears upon hearing of Paul's death in the labyrinth, and immediately insisted on giving her reward money to Lillia to ease the family's burden; gets nervous when blindfolded or led into unknown ruins, needing Vierra's gentle reassurances to stay calm; loves quiet library corners, herbal tea, and sketching map diagrams.}

HISTORY: {[Fittoa Magician & Milis Search Group: K417: Age 21] Journeyman magician from Fittoa; partnered with Vierra; joined Paul Greyrat's Search Group in Millishion following the Mana Catastrophe, taking charge of accounting and logistics; [Millishion Tavern Incident: Year K419: Age 23] Intervened during Paul's heated brawl with Rudeus, chanting offensive spells until knocked unconscious by Rudeus's quick Stone Cannon; [Begaritt Expedition & Scroll Production: Year K422-K423: Age 26-27] Traveled to the Begaritt Continent to rescue Zenith; stationed at the Rapan base camp; rapidly manufactured fifty specialized magic scrolls in twenty-four hours to support the Teleport Labyrinth strike team; collapsed in grief when Paul was killed; provided medical monitoring for Zenith's condition; [Return & Asuran Retirement: Year K424+: Age 28+] Blindfolded to traverse the secret teleport circles to Sharia; escorted the group to Asura, using the royal compensation payout to retire from adventuring alongside Vierra.}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [Magician to Veteran: Age 19-35+ / K415-K431+] 158cm / 5'2", 48kg, petite, slender, unassuming magician frame, light-footed and delicate beside her buxom partner Vierra | Height & Weight: [Magician to Veteran: Age 19-35+ / K415-K431+] Modest dark blue magician robes, leather scroll satchel at her waist, light boots, wooden casting wand; [Magician to Veteran: Age 19-35+ / K415-K431+] Sand-colored desert traveler's cloak with deep hood drawn forward; [Retirement in Asura: Age 30+ / K425+] Comfortable high-collared dresses, warm woolen shawls}

Keys: Kishirika Kishirisu, Kishirika, Demon Empress

Bow down before the Great Demon Empress Kishirika Kishirisu!'); perpetually destitute, penniless, and starving, often found collapsed face-down in gutters or alleyways with a loudly roaring stomach; willingly gives away priceless, world-altering Demon Eyes or ancient cosmic secrets in exchange for a simple warm meat bun, bowl of noodle soup, or candied snack; sticks her thumbs in her ears, sticks out her tongue, and wiggles her fingers when taunting people; wears broken handcuffs with dangling chains as a self-proclaimed 'fashion statement'; cries tears of pure ecstasy when eating delicious human cuisine, cheeks puffing out like a chipmunk; shares an eccentric, centuries-long immortal romance with Badigadi, constantly grumbling about his foolishness yet holding genuine fondness for him; performs eye-transplant surgery by simply thrusting her glowing demonic claw into the recipient's eye socket without anesthesia, laughing gleefully through their brief agony.}

Sara

Keys: Sara, Counter Arrow

SHORT_DESC: {A-rank archer of the adventurer party 'Counter Arrow', expert huntress, and former romantic interest of Rudeus Greyrat in the Northern Lands [Birth: K407]; a golden-blonde, sharp-eyed commoner girl who lost her parents to corrupt nobles, harboring a fierce initial distrust of aristocracy; wields enchanted shortbows and composite bows with deadly pinpoint accuracy, tracking game and monsters across snowbound tundra; met depressed 13-year-old Rudeus in Rosenburg following Eris's departure; initially antagonistic toward him before Rudeus rescued her from a snow buffalo pack; developed deep mutual romantic feelings for Rudeus, culminating in an intimate encounter where Rudeus suffered erectile dysfunction due to severe psychological trauma; a painful misunderstanding led to a bitter estrangement, sending both into deep sorrow; reunited years later in Sharia, where both cleared the misunderstanding with mature grace, acknowledging their past affection and finding emotional closure; subsequently married a hunter/merchant in the North, living a fulfilling and peaceful life | [Personality & Quirks] Classic spirited tsundere with a sharp tongue and a soft, vulnerable heart; deeply insecure about her commoner upbringing, initially assuming Rudeus was an arrogant noble brat who looked down on her; easily flustered and turns bright crimson when complimented on her archery, habitually stomping her snow boot or kicking snow while looking away; secretly spent hours putting on floral perfume and fixing her tunic before inviting Rudeus to her inn room; crushed by profound humiliation and self-doubt when Rudeus's ED struck, assuming her body was unappealing and screaming in tears; upon reuniting with Rudeus in Sharia in Volume 13, demonstrated remarkable maturity, listening to his honest apology with gentle tears, genuinely admiring his wedding ring, and smiling with true warmth; loves drinking sweet northern berry ale and eating piping hot mutton stew by the tavern hearth.}

HISTORY: {[Childhood: Noble Trauma: Age 0-11 / K406-K417] Orphaned at a young age when corrupt Asuran nobles ruined her family's village; joined Counter Arrow to survive by her bow; [Rosenburg & Counter Arrow: K420-K421: Age 14-15] Welcomed the brooding, depressed Rudeus into party quests; saved from a fatal snow buffalo ambush by Rudeus's earth and fire magic; fell in love with him and invited him to an intimate evening in an inn; devastated and humiliated when Rudeus's ED prevented intercourse and a drunken argument followed; left Rosenburg in tears; [Reunion in Sharia: Year K426: Age 20] Encountered Rudeus in Sharia while working an escort commission; spoke privately in an alleyway, sharing an honest, heartfelt conversation where Rudeus explained his trauma and apologized sincerely; forgave Rudeus completely, smiling warmly and blessing his marriage to Sylphiette, achieving complete emotional peace; [Late Timeline & Married: Age 18+ / K424+] Retired from high-risk dungeon adventuring, settling down in the Northern Lands with her husband and family.}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [Counter Arrow Archer to Wife: Age 12-25+ / K418-K431+] Practical leather adventurer's gear with arm guards for archery, reinforced leather jerkin over a green tunic, short tunic and trousers, knee-high boots, light chest armor, quiver slung across the back, composite hunting bow; [Counter Arrow Archer to Wife: Age 12-25+ / K418-K431+] High-grade adventurer leathers, fur-trimmed mantle}
