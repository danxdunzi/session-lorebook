Badigadi

Keys: Badigadi, Demon King of Immortality, Demon King of Wisdom

SHORT_DESC: {Immortal Demon King of the Demon Continent, Demon King of Wisdom, fiancé of Great Empress Kishirika Kishirisu, and wielder of the Fighting God Armor [Birth: Mythic Age]; a colossal, six-armed demon titan possessing obsidian-black diamond-hard skin, roaring belly laughter ('Gwahahaha!'), and absolute cellular immortality—capable of regenerating instantly from total vaporized obliteration; younger brother of Immortal Demon Lord Atoferatofe Rybak; despite his boisterous, hedonistic, and brute exterior, he is extraordinarily perceptive, well-read in ancient history, and possesses deep philosophical wisdom; enrolled as a special guest student at Ranoa Magic Academy, challenging Rudeus Greyrat to strike him with everything he had—laughing uproariously after Rudeus's supersonic Stone Cannon blew his torso to pieces before reforming in seconds; secretly bound by an unshakeable ancient life debt to Hitogami, who once saved him in primordial times; forced to fulfill this debt in the Biheiril Kingdom campaign by equipping the golden Fighting God Armor (assuming Rank 3 of the Seven Great Powers) and clashing in a titanic final duel against Rudeus in Magic Armor MK-Zero, Eris, Ruijerd, and Kalman II; ultimately disarmed and sealed in a magical earth barrier by Dragon God Orsted, maintaining unbroken respect and warm affection for Rudeus | [Personality & Quirks] Exuberant, larger-than-life, and completely unbothered by physical injury, death, or social taboos; punctuates sentences with booming, thunderous laughter that rattles tavern tables and window glass; genuinely loves drinking heavy demon liquor, eating roasted behemoth meat, and womanizing; beneath the boisterous buffoon facade lies sharp geopolitical intellect and millennia of cultural knowledge; treats combat as joyful performance and philosophical debate; harbors genuine love and protective devotion for Kishirika Kishirisu, enduring her eccentric tantrums with fond amusement; deeply honors vows, holding his blood debt to Hitogami as sacred even when it forces him to battle dear friends like Rudeus and Sandor; speaks in a hearty, informal, and booming voice; prone to crossing his top two arms while resting his bottom two on his hips and gesturing wildly with his middle pair.}

HISTORY: {[Immortal Demon King: Age Constant / Mythic Era to Present: Millennia] Equipped the cursed Fighting God Armor to battle Demon Dragon King Laplace; the clash tore the continent in two and shattered his consciousness across centuries; [Immortal Demon King: Age Constant / Mythic Era to Present: Millennia] Traveled to Sharia after hearing rumors of a wand-wielding prodigy; tested Rudeus's power in the academy courtyard, surviving a lethal Stone Cannon and declaring Rudeus a true champion; caroused in Sharia taverns and mentored swordsmen; [Immortal Demon King: Age Constant / Mythic Era to Present: Millennia] Summoned by Hitogami to collect the ancient debt; equipped the Golden Fighting God Armor, reaching Rank 3 of the Seven Great Powers; fought a monstrous, multi-phase war against Rudeus (MK-Zero), Eris, Ruijerd, and Kalman II; permanently sealed in an underground barrier cube by Orsted, smiling warmly at his comrades.}

Atoferatofe Rybak

Keys: Atoferatofe Rybak, Atofe, Immortal Demon Lord, Immortal Demon King

SHORT_DESC: {Immortal Demon King of the Necros Fortress on the Demon Continent, widow of North God Kalman I, and mother of North God Kalman II (Sandor von Grandeur) [Birth: Mythic Age]; daughter of ancient demon king Necros Lacross and older sister of Badigadi; possesses a viscous, blue-black slime-amorphous demon body granting instant regeneration from bisection, pulverization, or decapitation; notorious throughout the Six-Faced World for being exceptionally dim-witted, impulsive, and short-tempered, flying into violent rages whenever her intellect is mocked or contracts are questioned; revels in savage physical brawling above all worldly pleasures, forcing any warrior she defeats to sign eternal blood-vassalage contracts in her immortal undead legion; eternal bitter rival of Armored Dragon King Perugius Dola; challenged Rudeus's party at Necros Fortress, nearly killing them before being subdued by Dragon God Orsted; convinced by her son Sandor to ally with the Orsted Corporation in the Biheiril campaign, serving as an unstoppable, chaotic berserker on the battlefield | [Personality & Quirks] Wildly combative, hot-headed, and proud to the point of absurdity; possesses virtually zero diplomatic tact or strategic patience, solving every dispute by drawing her massive broadsword and challenging challengers to the death; laughs with a sharp, cackling demonic roar ('Hahaha! Die, maggot!'); extremely sensitive about her lack of academic intelligence, violently attacking anyone who uses long philosophical words; despite her terrifying brutality, she has a bizarrely naive and sentimental core—deeply cherishing memories of her late husband Kalman I (who courted her by defeating her repeatedly) and showing awkward maternal affection for Sandor; obsessively collects signed vassalage oaths on parchment, keeping them in fortress archives; hates Perugius with a fiery passion, spitting whenever his name is spoken; loves eating raw demonic beast meat and downing whole barrels of volcanic demon liquor.}

HISTORY: {[Immortal Demon Lord: Age Constant / Mythic Era to Present: Millennia] Ambushed Rudeus, Eris, and Zanoba; shattered into pieces by Rudeus's shotgun stone cannons and lightning before regenerating; brought to her knees in terror when Dragon God Orsted unleashed his dragon aura; [Immortal Demon Lord: Age Constant / Mythic Era to Present: Millennia] Mobilized her demonic royal guard after Sandor's familial mediation; smashed through Hitogami's apostle lines and fought North God Kalman III (Alec), turning the tide of the southern front.}

Pax Shirone

Keys: Pax Shirone, Pax, King Pax

SHORT_DESC: {Seventh Prince of the Shirone Kingdom, later King of the King Dragon Realm revolution, and tragic catalyst of the Six-Faced World's fate [Birth: K402 - Death: K424]; younger half-brother of Zanoba Shirone; initially introduced as a spoiled, tyrannical, and sadistic child prince who used Roxy Migurdia's likeness as a perverse obsession and held her hostage to trap Rudeus; exiled from Shirone following Rudeus and Zanoba's rebellion; enrolled at the King Dragon Realm Royal Magic Academy, where he matured into an exceptionally hardworking, capable military strategist and silent-magic pioneer; orchestrated a populist revolution in the King Dragon Realm alongside Queen Benedict, seeking to establish a modern egalitarian state and meritocracy; targeted by Hitogami's psychological manipulation and abandoned by his noble allies in the Shirone coup; overcome with despair when he realized his idealistic reforms were despised and that Benedict was trapped in political peril, he leaped to his death from the Shirone palace tower—a suicide that shattered Zanoba, haunted Rudeus, and drastically altered the geopolitical trajectory against Hitogami | [Personality & Quirks] In childhood, petulant, cruel, insecure, and deeply warped by royal neglect and Zanoba's monstrous physical favoritism; in adulthood, transformed into an austere, brilliant, and fiercely dedicated visionary bearing deep emotional scars; obsessed with earning genuine respect and romantic love; harbored genuine, passionate love for Benedict, treating her as his sole light in a corrupt world; carried an unyielding determination to prove that non-gifted ordinary humans could achieve greatness through rigorous discipline and tactical innovation; developed deep self-loathing regarding his past pathetic actions; habit of clenching his fists until his knuckles turned white when facing arrogant high nobility; spoke in crisp, formal, and bitterly sharp cadences in his mature years; his suicide was the tragic result of Hitogami exploiting his deepest psychological vulnerability: the desperate need to be needed.}

HISTORY: {[Shirone Palace Tyranny: Age 8-15 / K410-K417] Held Roxy hostage in the Shirone palace; outmaneuvered by Rudeus, Zanoba, and Gallus; stripped of royal standing and exiled to the King Dragon Realm; [King Dragon Realm Transformation: Age 16-21 / K418-K423] Studied military logistics and advanced barrier magic; married Benedict and fathered Pax Jr.; gained the backing of King Dragon Realm generals; [The Shirone Coup & Tragic Demise: Age 22 / K424] Led a military coup in Shirone to enact modern anti-noble reforms; betrayed by domestic generals under Hitogami's guidance; besieged in the royal palace; leaped from the parapets to his death despite Rudeus and Zanoba's desperate intervention, sending shockwaves through the world.}
