Lillia Greyrat

Keys: Lillia Greyrat, Lillia, Lilia

SHORT_DESC: {Head maid of the Greyrat household, second wife/concubine of Paul, and mother of Aisha Greyrat [Birth: K385]; former elite palace swordswoman of the Asuran Royal Guard and junior disciple of Water God Style alongside Paul; suffered a permanent tendon injury saving Princess Ariel from an assassin, entering Paul and Zenith's service in Buena Village; seduced Paul in emotional vulnerability, conceiving Aisha; saved from exile by 3-year-old Rudeus's impassioned defense, pledging lifelong maternal loyalty and service to him; managed the expanded Sharia estate with supreme administrative mastery | [Personality & Quirks] Maintained an early chilling dread toward infant Rudeus, observing him with cold suspicion when he stared at women's breasts, suspecting a demonic possession until his impassioned defense of her pregnancy won her eternal devotion; stands ramrod straight with hands folded over her apron, maintaining absolute maid decorum even in crisis; retains deadly Water God reflexes, capable of neutralizing intruders with kitchen knives or fireplace pokers despite her leg limp; trained Aisha relentlessly in elite maidcraft and bookkeeping, strictly scolding her whenever Aisha acted too casual; tenderly bathes, combs, and dresses Zenith every single morning, dedicated to preserving Zenith's dignity; secretly keeps a small wooden box of Paul's personal mementos, grieving in silent privacy; master brewer of Asuran aristocratic herbal teas, tonics, and medicinal infusions.}

HISTORY: {[Asuran Royal Guard: Age 18-23 / K402-K407] Served as an elite palace bodyguard in Ars; injured saving Princess Ariel; relocated to Buena Village as Paul and Zenith's maid; [Buena Village & Rudeus's Defense: K407-K414: Age 23-30] Became pregnant with Aisha; defended by toddler Rudeus when Zenith discovered the infidelity; became a beloved second mother to Rudeus, Norn, and Aisha; [Shirone Captivity & Rescue: K417-K419: Age 33-35] Teleported to Shirone during the Catastrophe; held hostage by Seventh Prince Pax; liberated when Rudeus and Zanoba intervened; [Sharia Estate Matriarch: Age 39+ / K423+] Reunited in Sharia; managed household logistics, servant training, and the care of telepathic Zenith until her peaceful old age.}

Norn Greyrat

Keys: Norn Greyrat, Norn

SHORT_DESC: {Second child of Paul and Zenith, younger sister of Rudeus, elder twin of Aisha, and wife of Ruijerd Superdia [Birth: K414]; mother of Luicelia Superdia and renowned canonical author of the monumental biography 'The Great Magician Rudeus'; struggled through childhood with inferiority living in the shadow of prodigy siblings Rudeus and Aisha; raised by Paul in the Fittoa Search Group, forming an intense bond with her father; overcame crippling social anxiety and a shut-in episode at Ranoa Academy to become the beloved Student Council President; married Ruijerd Superdia in Biheiril | [Personality & Quirks] Habitually puffs out her cheeks and pouts whenever teased by Rudeus or Aisha; profoundly hardworking, compensating for her lack of genius talent by studying late into the night, rewriting lecture notes dozens of times; kisses Zenith on the cheek every morning before leaving for school; initially terrified and resentful of Rudeus in Milishion after he fought Paul, screaming and throwing stones at him, but later recognized his tender care; suffered a severe shut-in breakdown in Volume 10 at Ranoa Academy, barricading herself in her dorm until Rudeus cried outside her door sharing his past-life pain; immense popularity as Student Council President, inspiring a devoted campus fan club ('Norn's Knight Guards'); fell deeply in love with Ruijerd, nursing him through the Superd plague in Biheiril with stubborn, unyielding devotion and proposing marriage regardless of race or age; an insightful diarist and chronicler.}

Aisha Greyrat

Keys: Aisha Greyrat, Aisha

SHORT_DESC: {Daughter of Paul and Lillia Greyrat, younger half-sister of Rudeus, and twin sister of Norn [Birth: K414]; multi-disciplinary genius in administration, strategy, logistics, household management, and social psychology; reared by Lillia to serve Rudeus as his personal maid and primary administrator; in divergent timelines without Rudeus, was destined to become the greatest magical prodigy of her century, inventing human lifespan-extension magic; in canon, directed estate stewardship and communications for Orsted Corporation; entered a controversial romantic relationship with nephew Ars Greyrat, eventually reconciling with family and bearing their son Leroy | [Personality & Quirks] Boundlessly energetic, cunning, and cheerful, masking complex emotional vulnerabilities regarding her identity and purpose; completely comfortable naked in front of Rudeus, casually walking into the bath unclad without hesitation, wriggling between his legs and playfully demanding he wash her back and rinse her hair, totally unbothered by nudity around him; discovered Rudeus's secret downstairs panty altar and playfully used that knowledge to blackmail him into letting her adopt and cultivate a dangerous Treant in the yard; effortless prodigy who masters cooking, sewing, arithmetic, gardening, magic, and stealth on the first attempt, leading to chronic boredom with ordinary tasks; initially patronizing toward Norn's slower pace, but grew fiercely protective of her sister; deeply loves cultivating flowers and tending gardens with Zenith, sharing apples and daffodils; secretly harbored fears of being viewed merely as a servant rather than a true family member until Rudeus affirmed her indispensable place in the home.}
