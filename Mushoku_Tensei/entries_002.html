Paul Greyrat

Keys: Paul Greyrat, Paul, Paul Notos Greyrat

SHORT_DESC: {Father of Rudeus, Norn, and Aisha, husband of Zenith and Lillia, and former garrison knight of Buena Village [Birth: K388 - Death: K424]; born Paul Notos Greyrat, high-born scion of House Notos Greyrat in Asura; fled home at twelve to escape noble corruption and an abusive father; an exceptionally gifted S-Rank swordsman proficient in dual-wielding across Sword God (lunges), Water God (parries), and North God (dirty tricks, feints, kicking dirt); charismatic, impulsive, and hedonistic founder of 'Fangs of the Black Wolf'; retired to Buena Village upon conceiving Rudeus with noble healer Zenith; a deeply loving yet profoundly flawed father | [Personality & Quirks] Possesses an unruly, hot-blooded temperament with a tendency to strike first in panic and suffer crushing guilt afterward, buying lavish reconciliation gifts; a notorious emotional lightweight with alcohol, breaking down into drunken sobs, hugging companions, and venting his deep insecurities about parenting; exchanged lecherous winks and bawdy jokes with young Rudeus behind Zenith's back, treating his son as an equal peer; harbored an inferiority complex toward toddler Rudeus's unnatural genius; woke Rudeus at dawn for rigorous sparring and jogs; deeply devoted to Zenith despite his Notos libido and his past affair with Lillia; in Milishion, fell into alcoholic despair and self-neglect while running the refugee search group; gave his life in the Teleport Labyrinth, pushing Rudeus clear of the Hydra's lethal strike.}

HISTORY: {[Age 0-12: Notos Noble Rebel / K381-K393] Reared in the aristocratic excess of Asura's House Notos; despised court politics and academic studies; fled at age twelve after striking his tutor; [Age 12-22: Fangs of the Black Wolf / K393-K403] Rose to S-Rank fame as a dual-wielding swordsman; conquered brutal dungeons across the Central Continent; had stormy romantic relationships with party members Elinalise and Ghislaine; reformed his reckless bachelor ways upon falling deeply in love with Zenith Latreia; disbanded the party when Zenith became pregnant with Rudeus, settling down in Buena Village; [Age 22-34: Buena Garrison Knight / K403-K415] Served as village knight; intimidated yet fiercely proud of toddler Rudeus's magical genius; had an affair with maid Lillia, which toddler Rudeus cleverly diffused, reconciling the family and integrating newborn Aisha; arranged Rudeus's tutoring post in Roa; [Age 34-39: Fittoa Search Group & Millis Reunion / K415-K420] Separated from his family during the Mana Catastrophe, landing in Millis with toddler Norn; founded the Fittoa Search Group, witnessing horrific refugee tragedies (slavery, massacres, death in volcanic zones) that drove him into severe depression and alcoholism; violently fought Rudeus in a Milis tavern when Rudeus arrived dressed well with Dead End, mistakenly believing Rudeus was living carefree while his family suffered; intervened by Geese Nukadia, leading to a tearful, deeply remorseful father-son reconciliation; [Age 39: Begaritt Labyrinth & The Sacrificial Fall / K420] Reunited with Rudeus, Elinalise, and Talhand in Rapan; led the raid into the Teleport Labyrinth to rescue Zenith; slashed through labyrinth guardians to the lowest chamber; during the climactic duel against the titanic Manatite Hydra, saw a lethal neck strike aimed at Rudeus's blind spot; kicked Rudeus out of the blast radius and took the Hydra's crushing maw directly, being bisected at the waist and killed instantly to ensure his son lived.}

Zenith Greyrat

Keys: Zenith Greyrat, Zenith

SHORT_DESC: {Mother of Rudeus and Norn, stepmother of Aisha, and devoted wife of Paul Greyrat [Birth: K390]; born Zenith Latreia, second daughter of Earl Latreia of the Holy Country of Milis; an Advanced-class Divine and Water magician who fled her aristocratic family's rigid religious expectations to seek freedom as an adventurer; party healer of 'Fangs of the Black Wolf'; exceptionally sweet, cheerful, and fiercely nurturing; retired in Buena Village upon conceiving Rudeus; accepted Lillia and newborn Aisha into the family with extraordinary magnanimity; rescued from the Teleport Labyrinth core into an apparent mute, vegetative state that masked profound Miko telepathic perception; lives peacefully in Sharia surrounded by loving family | [Personality & Quirks] Rebellious tomboy adventurer past, running away with a knapsack and cursing freely before settling down; playfully twisted Paul's ear whenever he ogled women or made lewd remarks; deeply devoted to the Millis faith while ignoring its ascetic hypocrisies, praying before meals with a small holy pendant; post-Begaritt, perceives her waking life as a continuous 'pleasant dream', enjoying the warm sun in the garden with Lillia, cultivating flowers (especially daffodils), and eating apples; receives a tender kiss on the cheek from Norn every morning before school; communicates wordlessly through telepathy with granddaughter Lara; her calm maternal gaze melts Rudeus's anxieties and childhood traumas.}

HISTORY: {[Age 0-15: Millis High Nobility / K383-K398] Reared in the devout, patriarchal House Latreia in Millis; rebelled against arranged aristocratic marriages and fled to the Central Continent; [Age 15-21: Fangs of the Black Wolf / K398-K404] Served as chief healer for Paul's S-rank adventurer party, mastering Advanced Healing and Detoxification; reformed Paul's philandering habits with her pure-hearted devotion; married Paul under the Millis Church's strict monogamy vow and retired upon becoming pregnant with Rudeus; [Age 21-33: Buena Village Motherhood / K404-K416] Reared Rudeus and Norn; warmly embraced Lillia and baby Aisha after toddler Rudeus mediated Paul's indiscretion; [Age 33-38: Teleport Labyrinth Crystal / K416-K421] Swept away by the Mana Catastrophe; teleported into the deepest sanctum of the Teleport Labyrinth in Begaritt; encased in a massive magic crystal beneath the lair of the Manatite Hydra in suspended animation for five years; [Age 38+: Rescue, Miko Telepathy & Peaceful Matriarch / K421+] Freed by Rudeus and Paul; emerged unable to chant or speak, leading observers to believe she suffered brain damage; later revealed by Dragon God Orsted and the Millis Telepathic Miko to possess powerful latent Miko telepathy, clearly perceiving her family's innermost thoughts and love; lived a joyful, tranquil life in the Sharia estate, tending the gardens and doting silently on her grandchildren Lara, Lucie, and Ars.}
