Lucy Greyrat

Keys: Lucy Greyrat, Lucy

SHORT_DESC: {Eldest child of Rudeus and Sylphiette Greyrat, elder sister of Sieghart, and eldest among all six Greyrat siblings [Birth: K423]; inherits human, elf, and beastman ancestry; gifted with remarkable intellect, self-discipline, and natural wind/healing magic aptitude; reared in Sharia under the loving care of Sylphiette, Roxy, and Eris; graduated from Ranoa Magic Academy at the top of her class; married Clive Grimoire (son of Cliff and Elinalise) and relocated to the Holy Country of Milis, becoming a prominent diplomatic figure | [Personality & Quirks] Earnest, proud, and deeply responsible as the eldest sibling of the Greyrat clan, always striving to set a flawless example for Lara, Ars, Sieg, Lily, and Chris; secretly hungers for Rudeus's approval and headpats, though intimidated by his towering legendary reputation; in toddlerhood, initially cried and hid behind Sylphie when Rudeus returned from Begaritt with a missing arm and gaunt frame, not recognizing him; loved running away naked from the bath while giggling, forcing Rudeus and Eris to chase her down the hall; grabbed Eris's strong hands to climb furniture, playfully pulling Eris's crimson hair while Eris gingerly supported her; developed a fierce childhood rivalry with Clive Grimoire over grades and magic, which blossomed into deep mutual romance; inherited small pointed elf ear tips from Sylphiette that blush when teased.}

HISTORY: {[Infancy & Early Years: Sharia: Age 0-10 / K423-K433] Born in Sharia shortly before the Begaritt expedition; her birth served as Rudeus's primary motivation to survive the labyrinth; reared alongside her siblings in a vibrant multi-wife home; formed an early bond with Clive Grimoire; [Adolescence: Ranoa Academy: Age 10-15 / K433-K438] Enrolled in Ranoa Magic Academy; initially struggled under the immense shadow of Rudeus's fame, striving tirelessly to prove her own worth without relying on parental favoritism; excelled in elemental and healing magic; [Adulthood: Marriage & Milis Settlement: Age 16+ / K439+] Confessed her feelings to childhood companion Clive Grimoire; married Clive and moved to the Holy Land of Milis, acting as a respected bridge between the Greyrat family, the Milis Church, and the Orsted Corporation; [Oldeus Alternate Fate: Age 0-1 / Alternate K423-K424] In the dark future of the Diary, taken as an infant by Sylphiette to Asura; survived the capital massacre and was raised in hiding after Sylphie's public execution.}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [Youth to Adult: Age 10-30+ / K433-K453+] University apprentice robes, wooden practice wand; [Late Timeline / Milis Matron: Age 20+ / K443+] High-class Milis scholarly attire, embroidered silver-white robes, modest jewelry, traveling attire for her move to Milis upon marriage to Clive}

Ars Greyrat

Keys: Ars Greyrat, Ars

Sieghart Saladin Greyrat

Keys: Sieghart Saladin Greyrat, Sieghart, Sieghardt, Death God

SHORT_DESC: {Second son of Rudeus Greyrat and second child of Sylphiette [Birth: K429]; born with bright emerald-green hair bearing the Laplace mana factor, initially terrifying Rudeus before being accepted and loved unconditionally; nicknamed 'Sieg'; named in honor of the invincible hero Siegfried and given the baptismal name 'Saladin' by Armored Dragon King Perugius Dola; possesses titanic physical strength, quiet humility, and an instinctive sense of justice; trained in the North God Style under Kalman II (Sandor von Grandeur), achieving the rank of North Emperor before succeeding Randolph Marianne as the Death God; wields the legendary Dragon-King Sword 'Dragon Slayer' with devastating mastery, becoming one of the most powerful martial figures of the post-Rudeus era | [Personality & Quirks] Born placid and calm, crying normally unlike Lara (who never cried) or Ars (who bawled at his father's hard chest); in childhood, suffered from a quiet inferiority complex, feeling alienated by his green hair and inability to excel at chant magic like his parents; deeply gentle by nature, hating unnecessary violence; inspired by Sandor's tales to become an ally of justice, secretly dressing in a hooded cloak and mask as the hero 'Moonlight'; reflexively bows his head and stammers when praised; extremely fond of sweet pastries baked by Sylphie; holds boundless respect for his father Rudeus and master Sandor, seeking to protect the weak without taking human lives.}

HISTORY: {[Infancy: The Green Hair Crisis: Age 0-2 / K427-K429] Born in Sharia with vivid green hair identical to Sylphiette's childhood color; Rudeus panicked fearing Hitogami's curse or the premature birth of Laplace; Orsted visited in secret at midnight to inspect the baby, confirming Laplace had not yet reincarnated; Lord Perugius summoned the family to Chaos Breaker, baptizing the infant in ancient divination water that glowed yellow (proving human nature) and bestowing the honorable name Saladin; [Youth: The Hero Complex & Sandor's Mentorship: Age 6-14 / K433-K441] Grew up quiet and contemplative, feeling detached from normal children due to his immense physical strength and green hair stigma; mentored by North God Kalman II (Sandor), who instilled in him the philosophy of true heroism and defensive justice; mastered North God swordsmanship, effortlessly deflecting lethal blows and neutralizing armed bandits without killing; [Adulthood: The Death God & Global Champion: Age 15+ / K442+] Traveled to the Shirone Republic and King Dragon Realm; intervened in geopolitical conflicts; inherited the legendary mantle of Death God from Randolph Marianne; stood as a monumental guardian of peace throughout the Six-Faced World.}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [Youth to Death God: Age 10-30+ / K437-K457+] Practical traveler's leathers, North God Style swordsgear, practice rapiers; [Death God Era: Age 20+] Black-and-gold Death God duster coat, reinforced steel shoulder guards, dual enchanted blades, Dragon King Sword}
