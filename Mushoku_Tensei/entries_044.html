Sauros Boreas Greyrat

Keys: Sauros Boreas Greyrat, Sauros, Lord of Roa, Sauros Boreas

SHORT_DESC: {Lord of the Fittoa Region, Mayor of the fortress city of Roa, and grandfather of Eris Boreas Greyrat [Birth: K350 - Death: K417]; an imposing, booming-voiced, and fiercely hot-tempered patriarch of House Boreas Greyrat; notoriously prone to shouting commands, violent disciplinary outbursts, and intimidating local nobles, yet possessed profound paternal warmth, incorruptible civic responsibility, and deep love for his granddaughter Eris; employed Rudeus Greyrat as Eris's live-in tutor, warmly embracing Rudeus's magical genius and family heritage; framed by the corrupt Minister Darius Silva Ganius for the Fittoa Metastasis Event and executed in Ars as a political scapegoat, dying with unbroken pride and defiant roars against his corrupt executioners | [Personality & Quirks] Incredibly loud, boisterous, aggressive, and short-fused, communicating almost entirely through thunderous roars and table-shattering fist slams; fiercely loyal to his territory and subjects, prioritizing the security of Roa's citizenry above high-court politics; possessed a soft, doting heart for Eris, spoiling her while simultaneously roaring encouragement at her sword practice; appreciated direct, honest, and courageous individuals, forming an instant bond of respect with young Rudeus; enjoyed consuming massive cuts of roasted meat and drinking heavy Asuran red wine from silver tankards; refused to bow or plead for mercy during his sham trial in Ars, shouting curses at Minister Darius until the executioner's blade fell.}

HISTORY: {[Lord of Fittoa & Roa: K370-K416: Age 20-66] Ruled Roa with an iron fist and fierce civic dedication; hired Rudeus Greyrat to educate and tame young Eris; hosted the celestial red orb monitoring station in Roa; [The Fittoa Disaster & Framing: K417: Age 67] Transported during the calamity; returned to find Roa destroyed; arrested in Ars under false charges of treason engineered by Minister Darius; publicly beheaded as a political scapegoat, cementing House Boreas's tragedy.}

Philip Boreas Greyrat

Keys: Philip Boreas Greyrat, Philip, Philip Boreas, Mayor of Roa

SHORT_DESC: {Mayor-adjoint of Roa, father of Eris Boreas Greyrat, son of Sauros Boreas Greyrat, and first cousin to Paul Greyrat [Birth: K380 - Death: K417]; a cunning, sharp-witted, and pragmatic aristocrat possessing refined courtly manners and deep political ambition; schemed to elevate his branch of House Boreas over the main house in Ars, orchestrating Rudeus's employment as Eris's tutor with the long-term goal of securing a political marriage between Rudeus and Eris; deeply loved his wife Hilda and daughter Eris, shielding them from the cutthroat machinations of Asuran high society; perished in the Conflict Zone alongside Hilda following the Fittoa Metastasis Event, having been teleported directly into a war-torn frontline | [Personality & Quirks] Shrewd, calculating, polite, and possessing a razor-sharp political intellect; contrasted starkly with his roaring father Sauros and feral daughter Eris by maintaining a calm, suave, and diplomatic exterior; loved to smoke fine Asuran tobacco from a long silver pipe while formulating multi-step noble schemes; deeply devoted husband who comforted Hilda through her grief over losing her elder sons to noble adoptions; treated Rudeus as an intellectual peer despite Rudeus's young age, sharing confidential political intelligence over wine; possessed genuine courage and protective love for his family, fighting to the last breath in the Conflict Zone to protect Hilda.}
