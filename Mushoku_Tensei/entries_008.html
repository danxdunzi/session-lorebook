Ghislaine Dedoldia

Keys: Ghislaine Dedoldia, Ghislaine, Sword King Ghislaine

SHORT_DESC: {Legendary Sword King of the Sword God Style, Beast race warrior of the Doldia Cat Tribe, and former bodyguard of Eris Boreas Greyrat [Birth: K380]; known across the world as the 'Black Dog' and the fearsome dark-furred Sword King; possesses an imposing, muscular, and athletic physique clad in minimal leather armor; bearer of a rare Demon Eye (the Demon Eye of Magic Perception) gifted by Kishirika Kishirisu, allowing her to see mana flows and concealed spells; former wild delinquent who abandoned her village, joining Paul Greyrat's S-Rank party 'Fangs of the Black Wolf'; completely illiterate and innumerate until hired by Philip Boreas Greyrat in Roa, where 7-year-old Rudeus taught her basic reading, writing, and arithmetic in exchange for advanced sword lessons; intensely loyal, blunt, and honorable, she served as Eris's martial mentor and maternal protector; an unstoppable whirlwind in melee combat wielding the enchanted Longsword of Light | [Personality & Quirks] Feral, blunt, and uncomplicated in thought, but deeply honorable and loyal to a fault; tail swishes rhythmically when alert and twitches nervously when agitated or forced to do bookwork; continually sniffs the air to detect enemies, incoming weather, or blood scents before anyone else; was completely unable to count beyond ten before Rudeus tutored her (counting on her fingers and looking utterly baffled when she ran out of fingers), developing an almost sacred reverence and eternal gratitude toward Rudeus for teaching her to read and do math; protective to the point of ferocity regarding Eris, acting as her fierce feline surrogate mother and baring her fangs at anyone who threatens her; eats massive hunks of roasted monster meat with her bare hands, chewing down to the bone and washing it down with fiery demon ale; light sleeper whose ears twitch at the faintest rustle, capable of waking and slicing an assassin in half in a single breath; terrified her delinquent niece Linia with a single terrifying glare when they met in later years; treated Paul with casual physical camaraderie in the old days, with Paul often joking that her rear and abs were as hard as solid granite.}

HISTORY: {[Great Forest Rebel: Age 0-10 / K380-K390] Rebelled against tribal traditions in Doldia Village; fled to become an adventurer; [Fangs of the Black Wolf: Age 20-35 / K400-K415] Formed the legendary party with Paul, Elinalise, and Talhand; developed a fierce rivalry and physical relationship with Paul; parted ways when the group disbanded; [Sword Sanctum & Roa Bodyguard: Age 36-37 / K416-K417] Studied under Sword God Gal Farion, attaining the rank of Sword King; hired by House Boreas Greyrat to protect young Eris; tutored Rudeus in advanced swordsmanship while learning arithmetic and language from him; [Mana Catastrophe & Asuran Search: Age 37-43 / K417-K423] Teleported to the Asuran royal palace during the incident; imprisoned by political factions before escaping; joined the royal knight corps to search for Eris and the Boreas family; [Reunion & Biheiril Campaign: Age 44-47+ / K424-K427+] Reunited with Eris after Eris became a Sword King; fought side-by-side with Eris and Rudeus in the battle against North God Kalman III and the Fighting God; served as chief martial instructor for the royal Asuran guards and the Greyrat children.}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [Sword King Career: Age 20-47+ / K400-K427+] 182cm / 6'0", 76kg, towering, overwhelmingly muscular, ripped amazon feline beastwoman physique; chiseled six-pack abs, dense warrior musculature, slender with rippling muscles, amazonian frame exposed by her revealing outfit; raced across rooftops, shattered a sword in midair and decapitated attackers with a shockwave that tore up the stone road; [Sword King Career: Age 20-47+ / K400-K427+] Veteran Sword King, still physically terrifying | Height & Weight: [Sword King Career: Age 20-47+ / K400-K427+] Revealing leather outfit exposing torso and limbs - upper half consists only of two leather straps over her breasts, never wears shirts; minimalist leather warrior harness and fur loincloth, sword belt with Hiramune katana; [Sword King Career: Age 20-47+ / K400-K427+] Revealing leather halter-top, leather combat shorts, high leather boots, black cape, greatsword on back; [Post-War / Seven Knights: Age 45+ / K425+] Seven Knights of Asura regalia worn over her traveling leathers, royal bodyguard cuirass}

Ariel Anemoi Asura

Keys: Ariel Anemoi Asura, Ariel, Second Princess Ariel

SHORT_DESC: {Second Princess and eventual supreme Queen of the Asura Kingdom [Birth: K405]; daughter of King Grabell and descendant of hero Kaunis Freean Asura; a peerless political prodigy possessing radiant platinum-blonde beauty, enchanting charismatic presence, and an iron Machiavellian intellect; survived dozens of childhood assassination attempts orchestrated by her corrupt half-brothers and Minister Darius Silva Ganius; saved during a palace garden assassination by a falling half-elf girl (Sylphiette), whom she recruited as her masked protector 'Silent Fitz'; exiled to the Magic University of Ranoa, where she served as beloved Student Council President and built an elite faction of foreign scholars, knights, and mages; backed by Rudeus Greyrat, Sylphiette, and Dragon God Orsted in a high-stakes return to Asura, systematically crushing Minister Darius's coup and ascending to the throne as Queen; an exceptionally capable, visionary monarch who ushered in a golden age of legal reform, educational expansion, and anti-Hitogami alliances | [Personality & Quirks] Publicly projects the image of an angelic, compassionate, and perfectly poised royal princess, but possesses a shrewd, calculating political mind and a private hedonistic streak; harbors a bisexual, promiscuous appetite in private chambers, taking pleasure with handsome knights like Luke and beautiful handmaidens alike; suffers from severe post-traumatic nightmares from surviving childhood poisons and assassin blades, occasionally waking drenched in cold sweat and needing Sylphiette to hold her hand until her pulse steadies; habitually conceals intense anxiety by biting the side of her thumb behind a silk fan or lace handkerchief; a peerless actress capable of summoning tears, trembling vulnerability, or regal fury at will to sway noble assemblies; deeply codependent on Sylphiette (Fitz), feeling a bittersweet pang of possessiveness when Sylphie married Rudeus, yet warmly blessing their happiness; possesses nerves of absolute steel when cornered, facing down Water God Reida Lia and First Prince Grabell with an unyielding imperial glare; loves drinking black tea sweetened with rare flower honey and eating Asuran candied fruit.}

HISTORY: {[Palace Childhood: Assassination Gauntlet: Age 0-14 / K405-K419] Raised in the treacherous court of Ars; targeted repeatedly by poison and assassins; saved by Sylphiette during the Fittoa Incident; formed an unbreakable emotional reliance on Sylphiette; [Ranoa Exile & Political Organization: K419-K423: Age 14-18] Fled to Ranoa with Sylphiette and Luke; established the Ariel Faction; recruited foreign nobility and secured the backing of the Magic Guild; [The Silver Palace Coronation Campaign: Age 20 / K425] Formed a historic compact with Dragon God Orsted through Rudeus; returned to Ars for the royal banquet; exposed Minister Darius's treason and Water God Reida Lia's coup; ascended the throne as Queen of Asura; [Reign & Global Alliances: Age 21+ / K426+] Established universal elementary education; curbed noble corruption; provided state financing, naval fleets, and intelligence networks for the Orsted Corporation; mother of Prince Edward (her sovereign consort being Luke Notos Greyrat).}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [Princess to Queen: Age 15-30+ / K420-K435+] styled in elaborate, flawless imperial curls draping over shoulders | Eyes: [Princess to Queen: Age 15-30+ / K420-K435+] Ranoa Academy uniform worn with elegance, elegant tailored purple academy princess gown with ermine fur collar and silver tiara; [Coronation & Queen Era: K425+: Age 20+] Queen regalia in white and gold beneath a breathtaking coronation gown of purple velvet with pure gold embroidery, the Imperial Asuran Crown, holding the royal sceptre}

Luke Notos Greyrat

Keys: Luke Notos Greyrat, Luke

SHORT_DESC: {Scion of House Notos Greyrat, knight bodyguard and sovereign consort of Queen Ariel Anemoi Asura, and first cousin of Rudeus Greyrat [Birth: K405]; son of Pilemon Notos Greyrat and nephew of Paul Greyrat; an impeccably dressed, handsome nobleman renowned for his charming womanizing habits and courtly etiquette; an accomplished swordsman proficient in Water God Style parries and North God trickery, wielding an enchanted rapier; served Princess Ariel with absolute, unyielding devotion from early childhood, willingly sharing her foreign exile at Ranoa; conflicted by hidden family obligations to House Notos and manipulated by Hitogami's whisperings during the Asuran succession crisis; ultimately broke free of Hitogami's mental trap to stand beside Ariel, marrying her as her official consort and fathering First Prince Edward; a flawed, prideful, yet fundamentally noble knight whose devotion ensured Ariel's survival | [Personality & Quirks] Unabashed playboy who flirts effortlessly with maids and female academy students, yet harbors a lifelong, pure devotion to Ariel as the only woman who truly owns his heart; obsessed with his appearance, constantly checking his reflection in pocket mirrors and running a silver comb through his wavy golden hair; harbored an intense competitive jealousy and inferiority complex toward Rudeus, initially suspecting Rudy of being a lecherous fraud trying to seduce Fitz and upstage him; challenged Rudeus to a duel in Volume 10 over Sylphie's hand and was easily bested, growing sullen before earning Rudy's respect; pushed to severe mental anguish when Hitogami invaded his dreams, threatening the execution of his father Pilemon; broke down in genuine tears before Rudeus in a fortress cellar, confessing his terror and doubts before bravely choosing Ariel over Hitogami; loves fine Asuran vintages, wears expensive floral cologne, and is fiercely proud of the Notos Greyrat swordsmanship lineage.}

HISTORY: {[Guardian Knight to Consort: Age 15-30+ / K419-K434+] Married Queen Ariel as her consort, fathering Crown Prince Edward; governed royal security and maintained close diplomatic ties with Rudeus and the Greyrat family; [Oldeus Alternate Fate: Age 21 / Alternate K425] In the Diary's dark timeline, remained loyal to Ariel in her ill-fated coup, slain in battle defending her before her public execution.}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [Guardian Knight to Consort: Age 15-30+ / K419-K434+] above average, intact, ~6", matured | [Guardian Knight to Consort: Age 15-30+ / K419-K434+] Ranoa Academy uniform, white-and-purple knight doublet with rapier at hip, student council member; [Guardian Knight: Age 15-21 / K419-K425] Asuran knight armor with the Notos seal for combat, polished silver-and-gold Asuran knight plate, feathered cavalier hat, fine nobleman's tunic and trousers, Guardian Knight regalia; [Post-War & Royal Consort: Age 22+ / K426+] Apostle of Hitogami garb briefly}
