Nina Falion

Keys: Nina Falion, Nina

Reida Lia

Keys: Reida Lia, Reida, Water God Reida, Water God

SHORT_DESC: {The supreme Water God of the contemporary era, grandmother and master of Isolde Cluel, and apex master of the Water God Style [Birth: K360 - Death: K423]; bearer of the hereditary title 'Reida Lia', passed down to the reigning grandmaster of the style; an elderly, slight woman possessing absolute martial omniscience, having elevated Water God counters to the ultimate secret art: the 'Deprivation Field' (Blade of Deprivation)—a localized domain where any offensive movement or hostile intent is instantly met with an unavoidable, fatal parry-counter; served as the ultimate hidden trump card for Minister Darius Silva Ganius in the Asuran royal succession crisis; unleashed her Deprivation Field during the Silver Palace coronation banquet, paralyzing Rudeus, Sylphiette, Eris, and Ghislaine; ultimately breached and annihilated in a single stroke by Dragon God Orsted, dying with serene martial pride | [Personality & Quirks] Calm, polite, grandmotherly, and deceptively frail in appearance, speaking in the gentle, creaky tone of an elderly village elder; completely devoid of typical martial arrogance, treating deadly duels with the casual ease of pouring afternoon tea; harbors a pragmatic, cynical worldview shaped by decades of Asuran court intrigue; deeply fond of her granddaughter Isolde, wanting to secure a prestigious noble future for her house; harbors no personal malice toward her targets, viewing assassination as a contracted duty; has a signature habit of leaning gently on her walking cane while observing battlefields with tranquil, half-closed eyes; smiled serenely upon witnessing Orsted's god-tier strike, finding peace in seeing a blade that surpassed her ultimate technique.}
