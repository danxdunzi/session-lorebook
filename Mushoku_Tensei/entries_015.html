Gal Farion

Keys: Gal Farion, Sword God Gal, Sword God

SHORT_DESC: {The supreme Sword God (predecessor to Gino Britz), master of the Holy Land of Swords dojo, and tutor of Eris Boreas Greyrat and Nina Falion [Birth: K361 - Death: K427]; Rank 6 of the Seven Great World Powers; the pinnacle master of the Sword God Style, having brought the 'Longsword of Light' and 'Blade of Deprivation' to absolute lethal perfection; an arrogant, ruthless, and brutally straightforward swordsman who believes purely in absolute speed, decisive first-strike lethality, and natural martial dominance; trained Eris into a Sword King after recognizing her feral combat genius; challenged by his nephew and apprentice Gino Britz for the title of Sword God after setting the condition that Gino could only marry Nina if he surpassed him; defeated by Gino's evolved, zero-anticipation Longsword of Light, losing his title; recruited as an apostle by Hitogami during the Biheiril campaign, setting a deadly mountain ambush against Rudeus, Eris, and Ruijerd; slain in a ferocious duel by Eris and Ruijerd, dying as a pure, uncompromising swordsman | [Personality & Quirks] Bluntest, most aggressive martial master in the world; values nothing except cutting speed, combat instinct, and kill efficiency; constantly lounges with one leg slung over his training chair, spitting straw and chewing jerky while critiquing students with savage insults; refuses to teach students who lack killer instinct, telling them to go join the Water God style if they want to live long; deeply prideful, yet possesses genuine respect for true monsters of combat like Orsted and Eris; harbored fatherly care for Nina beneath his harsh exterior, deliberately setting the bar impossibly high to ensure only the strongest warrior could protect her; speaks in a gruff, barking, dialect-heavy tone; never draws his blade for show—every draw is an instant lethal execution strike.}

HISTORY: {[Ascent to Sword God: Age 20-25 / K381-K386] Defeated the previous Sword God in single combat at the Holy Land of Swords; established the highest peak of Sword God Style supremacy; [Mentoring Eris & Nina: Age 58-63 / K419-K424] Accepted feral Eris as a direct disciple; polished her instincts into a Sword King; tested Rudeus's reputation; [Loss of Title to Gino Britz: Age 65 / K426] Challenged by Gino Britz; overwhelmed and defeated by Gino's unprecedented velocity, relinquishing the Sword God mantle; [The Biheiril Ambush & Death: Age 66 / K427] Manipulated by Hitogami; ambushed Rudeus's party in the Biheiril mountains alongside North God Kalman III; slain in a master-class duel by Eris and Ruijerd Superdia.}

Soldat Heckler

Keys: Soldat Heckler, Soldat, Stepped Leader, Thunderbolt

SHORT_DESC: {Leader of the S-Rank adventurer clan 'Stepped Leader', veteran northern mercenary, and sworn older brother figure to Rudeus Greyrat [Birth: K395]; a gruff, sharp-tongued, and fiercely dependable northern swordsman wielding a heavy greatsword with Sword God and North God mastery; encountered teenage Rudeus during the 'Quagmire' era in Rosenburg, initially clashing with him over Rudeus's depressed, emotionally numb persona; forcefully intervened during Rudeus's suicidal downward spiral following his failed intimacy with Sara, dragging Rudeus to a tavern, drinking through the night, taking him to a pleasure house, and helping him confront his emotional trauma; forged an unbreakable bond of brotherhood with Rudeus, teaming up on high-tier dragon hunts and snow drake raids; provided mercenary support, regional connections, and logistical muscle for the Orsted Corporation across the Northern Territories | [Personality & Quirks] Outwardly cynical, loud-mouthed, and foul-tempered, but secretly possessive of the biggest heart and sharpest emotional intuition among northern adventurers; hates people who bottle up their pain and play the martyr; speaks in rough northern dialect laced with colorful profanity; has a signature habit of slapping comrades hard across the shoulder blades and buying rounds of cheap ale when morale is low; deeply loyal to his Stepped Leader clan members, treating them as blood family; loves high-stakes gambling, northern tavern brawls, and smoking pungent dried pipe weed; proved to be the pivotal emotional anchor who prevented teenage Rudeus from ending his life during his deepest depression in Rosenburg; fiercely proud of Rudeus's eventual marriage and family, crying drunken tears of joy at his friend's happiness.}

HISTORY: {[Northern Mercenary Ascent: Age 16-24 / K411-K419] Formed and led 'Stepped Leader', achieving S-Rank status in the Northern Lands through dragon hunts; [Rescuing Rudeus from Despair: Age 26 / K421] Confronted suicidal Rudeus in Rosenburg; dragged him through emotional recovery; formed a celebrated dragon-hunting tag team; [Lifelong Alliance with Greyrat Clan: Age 28+ / K423+] Reunited with Rudeus during university and Asuran campaigns; mobilized northern mercenary networks to support the Orsted Corporation against Hitogami's covert forces.}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [Early Adventuring: Age 18-24 / K413-K419] 180cm, 78kg, lean and rugged northern warrior; [S-Rank Leader & Quagmire Mentoring: Age 25-30 / K420-K425] 182cm / 6'0", 84kg, rugged, heavy-set, and broad-shouldered northern swordsman physique; thick barrel chest, dense arm musculature forged from wielding heavy claymores in sub-zero blizzards; [Veteran Mercenary Commander: Age 31+ / K426+] 182cm, 86kg, scarred, massive veteran soldier frame | Height & Weight: [Adult: Age 25+ / K420+] 182cm / 6'0", 84-86kg | Penile: [Adult: Age 20+ / K415+] Average to slightly above average, rugged and functional northern build, regular patron of northern brothels | Skin: [Mercenary Career: Age 18-35+ / K413-K430+] Fair northern complexion, weather-roughened from frostbite and sun glare, stubbled jaw | Face: [Mercenary Career: Age 18-35+ / K413-K430+] Hard, craggy jaw with a distinctive scar across the left cheek; cynical smirk that turns into a broad, toothy grin when drinking | Hair: [Mercenary Career: Age 18-35+ / K413-K430+] Short, messy sandy-blonde hair, spiked slightly from wind and helmet wear | Eyes: [Mercenary Career: Age 18-35+ / K413-K430+] Sharp, cynical steel-blue eyes that soften with brotherly warmth toward comrades | Distinguishing Traits: Heavy greatsword slung across back; distinct smell of northern pipe weed and dark ale; loud, booming northern laugh | Outfits by Timeline: [Rosenburg Era: Age 25-27 / K420-K422] Heavy fur-lined leather and steel-reinforced coat over chainmail, studded leather vambraces, heavy fur boots, traveling cloak bearing the Stepped Leader crest; [Mercenary Commander Era: Age 30+ / K425+] High-grade dark steel cuirass over heavy wool coat, twin greatsword straps}

Gallus Cleaner

Keys: Gallus Cleaner, Gallus, The Cleaner

SHORT_DESC: {North Saint of the North God Style, ruthless international smuggler captain, and covert apostle of Hitogami [Birth: K385 - Death: K418]; a cunning, handsome, and deceitful human mercenary operating extensive illicit beastfolk and human trafficking syndicates between the Demon Continent, the Great Forest, and the Holy Country of Milis; approached Dead End in Zant Port under the guise of an affable, well-connected merchant-fixer, offering sea passage across the ocean in exchange for liberating contraband goods from customs; revealed to be an apostle of Hitogami tasked with capturing Sacred Beast beastfolk children in Doldia Village and orchestrating the assassination of Dead End; fought Ruijerd Superdia in a vicious, high-speed duel in the burning port warehouse, employing North God irregular footwork, blinding powder, and throwing daggers before being impaled and slain by Ruijerd's spear | [Personality & Quirks] Projects an oily, charming, and thoroughly persuasive gentleman-merchant persona; smiles easily while calculating the market value of human lives; possesses cold, calculating sociopathy masked beneath polite compliments and professional business jargon; strictly loyal to Hitogami's dream visions, viewing the Man-God as an infallible divine patron for his criminal empire; masters the North God philosophy of 'any tactic that yields victory is righteous', shamelessly using hostages, concealed poisons, and blinding sand; habit of flipping a gold coin between his knuckles while negotiating illicit deals; speaks in smooth, theatrical cadences that instantly put naive clients at ease; underneath his polished facade lies an arrogant, sadistically cruel killer who relishes breaking the spirits of prideful warriors.}

HISTORY: {[Smuggling Syndicate & North God Training: Age 15-29 / K400-K414] Attained North Saint ranking; established the 'Cleaner' smuggling network spanning Zant Port, the Great Forest, and Milis; [Hitogami's Apostle Directives: Age 32 / K417] Received dream instructions to abduct beastfolk children from Doldia Village and ambush Dead End; [The Battle of Zant Port & Death: Age 33 / K418] Posed as Dead End's savior at Zant Port; staged a hostage trap in the port warehouse; confronted Ruijerd Superdia in single combat; defeated by Ruijerd's Devil Eye and lethal spear thrust, ending his criminal enterprise.}

The Old Man (Future Rudeus Greyrat)

Keys: The Old Man, Future Rudeus Greyrat, Old Man, Future Rudeus, Oldeus, Oldeus Diary, Oldeus's Diary, The Diary

SHORT_DESC: {Rudeus Greyrat from fifty years in a catastrophic alternate timeline ('Oldeus'), tragic martyr, master of gravity and temporal magic, and savior of the Greyrat bloodline [Birth: K407 - Death: K423]; arrived through a desperate, one-way temporal rift into present Rudeus's Sharia basement to avert the horrific dystopia engineered by Hitogami; in his timeline, Hitogami tricked him into opening the basement cellar, allowing a demon rat to infect pregnant Roxy with lethal Petrification Syndrome; unable to save Roxy or their unborn child, he fell into suicidal alcoholism, drug abuse, and domestic violence; Sylphiette left him and was publicly executed in Ars during a botched coup, Eris sacrificed her life taking a lethal blow from Demon Lord Atoferatofe to protect him, and Zanoba, Aisha, Julie, and Ginger were massacred in a basement raid; spent decades roaming the world as a broken, ruthless avenger—mastering voiceless gravity manipulation, electric magic, dragon ruins, and temporal equations; sacrificed his internal organs, stomach, and life force to tear open a rift in space-time, handing his bloodstained Diary to young Rudeus to correct his past before dying with a peaceful smile | [Personality & Quirks] Haunted, emotionally shattered, cynical, and ruthlessly pragmatic, yet harboring an overwhelming, desperate love for his lost family; speaks with a raspy, blood-choked voice filled with fifty years of agonized regret; completely devoid of the present Rudeus's perverted humor, replaced by grim, ascetic determination; deeply despised himself for his failures as a husband, father, and brother; wept uncontrollable tears upon seeing the living, pregnant Roxy and Sylphiette one last time; possessed peerless combat instinct, having fought every apostle and god across the globe alone; his final act was delivering clear, life-saving advice: 'Read the diary, write a letter to Eris, and trust the Dragon God Orsted.'}

HISTORY: {[The Cataclysm of Roxy & Household Collapse: Age 16-17 / Alternate K423-K424] Opened the cellar door under Hitogami's advice; lost Roxy to Magic Stone Disease; descended into alcoholism and violence; lost Sylphiette to Asuran execution and Eris to Atoferatofe's blade; [Decades of Vengeance & Forbidden Magic: Age 20-60 / Alternate K427-K467] Ruthlessly hunted Hitogami's apostles across all continents; mastered gravity magic and lightning; decoded ancient Dragon World temporal matrices; [The Temporal Jump & Death: Age 63 body / Present K423] Burned away his internal organs and life force to cast forbidden time-reversal magic; materialized in present Rudeus's basement; delivered the Diary, guided Rudeus to Orsted, and passed away peacefully in present Rudeus's arms; buried with deep reverence beside Paul's memorial.}
