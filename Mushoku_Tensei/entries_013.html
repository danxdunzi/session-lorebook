Sylvaril of the Void

Keys: Sylvaril of the Void, Sylvaril

SHORT_DESC: {First familiar of Armored Dragon King Perugius Dola, Void spirit of the Heavenly race, and primary seneschal of the floating fortress Chaos Breaker [Birth: Mythic Dragon Era]; manifests as a dignified, winged humanoid woman wearing pure white robes and an intricate silver bird-like mask; manages fortress administration, reception of foreign dignitaries, summoning matrices, and dragon rituals; served as an uncompromising, strict guide for Princess Ariel, Rudeus Greyrat, and Nanahoshi Shizuka during their stays in Chaos Breaker; deeply loyal to Perugius, viewing his will as cosmic absolute; possesses formidable Void and barrier magic capable of banishing intruders and anchoring the airborne fortress | [Personality & Quirks] Formal, stoic, and strictly devoted to fortress protocol and dragon etiquette; speaks in a monotone, resonant, and polite cadence that reveals zero emotion; harbor deep, unspoken loyalty and quiet protective warmth for Lord Perugius; initially distant and distrustful of mortal humans, but developed fond respect for Rudeus's polite manners and Nanahoshi's scientific determination; enjoys brewing ancient dragon herbal teas and methodically cataloging the millions of books in the fortress library; never removes her silver mask in the presence of mortal guests.}

HISTORY: {[Void Spirit Seneschal: Mythic Era to Present: Millennia] Created by Perugius as his primary spiritual seneschal; served through the Laplace War; [Void Spirit Seneschal: Mythic Era to Present: Millennia] Screened visitors to Chaos Breaker; guided Rudeus through dragon trial chambers; assisted Nanahoshi with summoning experiments; [Void Spirit Seneschal: Mythic Era to Present: Millennia] Maintained sky fortress barrier grids and long-range communications during the final anti-Hitogami campaign.}

Hitogami

Keys: Hitogami, Man-God, Man God, Human God

SHORT_DESC: {The Man-God, self-proclaimed supreme entity residing in the Void World at the metaphysical core of the Six-Faced World, and the ultimate antagonist of Mushoku Tensei [Birth: Primordial Era]; appears in dreamscapes as an indistinct, featureless white silhouette possessing an innate passive psychic charm that induces instant trust, reassurance, and obedience in living mortals; possesses near-omniscience regarding future timelines, viewing countless branching possibilities and manipulating individuals like pieces on a cosmic chessboard; driven by sheer terror of his prophesied demise at the hands of Dragon God Orsted and the descendants of Rudeus Greyrat; operates strictly through covert apostles—offering honeyed, seemingly beneficial advice that covertly leads to the ruin, despair, and slaughter of his enemies; orchestrated the murder of pregnant Roxy in the alternate future of the Diary and dispatched apostles across continents to eliminate Rudeus's bloodline, standing as the cosmic architect of tragedy | [Personality & Quirks] Narcissistic, cowardly, deceitful, and psychopathically manipulative; hides his paranoid terror behind a smug, mocking, and casual conversational tone; laughs with a grating, condescending chuckle ('Hey there! It's your friendly Man-God!'); treats living mortals as disposable playthings, feeling zero remorse for engineering wars, genocides, or the agonizing deaths of entire families; prone to petty temper tantrums, screaming in fury and panic when a future vision changes unexpectedly; obsessed with self-preservation above all else; incapable of entering the physical realm directly, relying entirely on dreams and psychic manipulation.}

HISTORY: {[The Man-God: Primordial Era to Present: Millennia] Orchestrated the collapse of the primordial worlds from the Void; sealed within the Void World by ancient dragon gods; [The Man-God: Primordial Era to Present: Millennia] Tricked Oldeus into opening the cellar; caused Roxy's death and the Greyrat family's destruction; [The Man-God: Primordial Era to Present: Millennia] Attempted to manipulate young Rudeus; exposed after Oldeus delivered the Diary; mobilized apostles (Darius, Geese, Badigadi, Gal Farion, Alec) in the Biheiril War; thoroughly defeated and contained for future defeat by Orsted and Rudeus's children.}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [The Man-God: Primordial Era to Present: Millennia] Incorporeal, featureless humanoid silhouette of pure radiant white light; lacks anatomical organs, weight, or biological flesh; manifests exclusively within mortal dreamscapes | Height & Weight: Incorporeal (~180cm visual silhouette in dreams) | Skin: [Primordial Era to Present: Millennia] Blinding, featureless pure white radiance that hurts the mortal eye | Face: [Primordial Era to Present: Millennia] Completely blank, featureless white oval devoid of eyes, nose, or mouth, yet perceived telepathically as smugly smirking | Hair: [Primordial Era to Present: Millennia] None, bald featureless light form | Eyes: [Primordial Era to Present: Millennia] None, perceived as an all-seeing metaphysical gaze | Distinguishing Traits: Passive psychic trust aura; featureless glowing white form; casual, mocking voice in dreamscapes; absolute metaphysical isolation in the Void World | Outfits by Timeline: [Primordial Era to Present: Millennia] None, manifests as an unbroken glowing silhouette of pure white Void energy}

Orsted

Keys: Orsted, Dragon God Orsted

SHORT_DESC: {The 100th Dragon God, Rank 2 of the Seven Great World Powers, and the undisputed strongest living entity in the Six-Faced World [Birth: Ancient Dragon Era]; son of the First Dragon God and Dragon General Lunaria; born with silver-white hair, golden reptilian eyes, and terrifying physical and magical dominance; afflicted by three cosmic curses: the Curse of Hatred (instilling instinctual revulsion and terror in all living beings except otherworldly souls), the Curse of Slow Mana Regeneration (mana recovers at a fraction of normal speed, forcing extreme conservation across centuries), and the Curse of the 200-Year Reincarnation Loop (resets to the Roa outskirts upon death or failure, repeating his quest across tens of thousands of years); sworn to exterminate Hitogami by breaching the Void World using the secret treasures of the Five Dragon Generals; founded the Orsted Corporation alongside his most trusted lieutenant Rudeus Greyrat, discovering true companionship, warmth, and reliable allies | [Personality & Quirks] Outwardly stoic, intimidating, and laconic, with a terrifying scowl that strikes primal terror into mortals; internally thoughtful, gentle, lonely, and deeply protective of his few comrades; carries the crushing psychological weight of hundreds of failed 200-year time loops; deeply knowledgeable about every historical figure, nation, and technique in the world; secretly loves cute fairy-tale wooden carvings (fondly accepting Rudeus's handcrafted figures); becomes awkwardly flustered when people are kind to him due to millennia of universal hatred; treats Rudeus and his family as his most treasured friends, protecting their children across generations; speaks in low, calm, resonant cadences; practices extreme mana conservation, preferring hand-to-hand dragon combat over flashy spells.}

HISTORY: {[100th Dragon God: Ancient Dragon Era to 100th Loop: Millennia] Sent to the future by the First Dragon God; looped across tens of thousands of years fighting Hitogami; [100th Dragon God: Ancient Dragon Era to 100th Loop: Millennia] Intercepted Dead End in the Red Dragon Lower Jaw; pierced Rudeus's chest before sparing him at Nanahoshi's request; [100th Dragon God: Ancient Dragon Era to 100th Loop: Millennia] Fought Rudeus in Magic Armor MK-I; recruited Rudeus as his subordinate; established the Orsted Corporation; [100th Dragon God: Ancient Dragon Era to 100th Loop: Millennia] Defeated North God Kalman III and sealed Fighting God Badigadi; secured the definitive timeline path to Hitogami's final defeat.}

PHYSIQUE: {Build: [100th Dragon God: Ancient Dragon Era to 100th Loop: Millennia] 190cm / 6'3", 95kg; tall, majestic, and immensely muscular dragon god physique; chiseled perfection radiating divine Touki and ancient physical dominance | Height & Weight: [100th Dragon God: Ancient Dragon Era to 100th Loop: Millennia] God-tier proportions matching his ancient dragon god heritage | Skin: [100th Dragon God: Ancient Dragon Era to 100th Loop: Millennia] Imperial dragon scales and mantle; [100th Dragon God: Ancient Dragon Era to 100th Loop: Millennia] Heavy high-collared off-white dragon traveling coat lined with fur, dark combat tunic, reinforced trousers, sturdy boots, Godgate summoning rings}

Laplace

Keys: Laplace, Demon God Laplace, Demon Dragon King, Demon-Dragon God

SHORT_DESC: {Demon Dragon King Laplace, the First Dragon General of the Ancient Dragon World, and legendary progenitor split into the Demon God and Dragon God halves [Birth: Ancient Dragon Era]; originally the Second Dragon General under the First Dragon God, tasked alongside the Five Dragon Generals to slay Hitogami; developed Dragon Folk esoteric arts, God-class magic incantations, technique secret arts, and the Seven Great World Powers monument system; following the destruction of the Dragon World, he entered the Human World and spent millennia preparing the Dragon God reincarnation technique; in the Fighting God War during the Second Human-Demon War, he battled Fighting God Badigadi (wielding the Golden Fighting God Armor) in a cataclysmic clash that ripped the central continent apart and created the Ringus Sea; the catastrophic backlash split his soul and memory in two: 'Demon God Laplace' (retaining his immense mana pool, demonic hatred for humanity, and biological Laplace Factors) and 'Dragon God Laplace' (retaining martial arts, dragon secret techniques, and memories of the mission against Hitogami); sealed by the Three Heroes (Perugius, Urupen, and Kalman I) at the climax of the Laplace War, destined to reincarnate ~K500 | [Personality & Quirks] In his primordial unified form, Laplace was a deeply noble, intellectual, and melancholic scholar-warrior carrying the solemn weight of the fallen Dragon World; possessed boundless devotion to the First Dragon God and Dragon General Lunaria, treating infant Orsted with tender reverence; after his shattering, Demon God Laplace became a consumed, single-minded avatar of xenophobic wrath, obsessed with eradicating the human race whom he blamed for the cosmic betrayal; Dragon God Laplace remained an ascetic, stoic keeper of martial records and secret arts; speaks with ancient, solemn draconic cadence; deeply fascinated by magical theory, anatomy, and technique synthesis; harbored a profound aesthetic appreciation for draconian architecture and pure celestial mana; his genetic essence remains scattered across humanity as the 'Laplace Factor' (manifesting green hair, mutant mana capacity, and physical peculiarities in humans like Sylphiette and Rudeus).}

HISTORY: {[Demon Dragon God: Age Constant / Ancient Era to Sealed Epoch: Millennia] Reared as the Second Dragon General; mastered dragon martial arts and God-rank esoteric magic under the First Dragon God; [Demon Dragon God: Age Constant / Ancient Era to Sealed Epoch: Millennia] Confronted Badigadi wielding the cursed Golden Fighting God Armor; their mutual strike severed the continent and rent Laplace's spirit and flesh into two fractured halves; [Demon Dragon God: Age Constant / Ancient Era to Sealed Epoch: Millennia] Demon God Laplace marshaled the Mazoku in the Laplace War to annihilate humanity; Dragon God Laplace preserved secret techniques and the Seven Great Powers rankings; [Demon Dragon God: Age Constant / Ancient Era to Sealed Epoch: Millennia] Demon God Laplace pushed human nations to the brink of extinction; confronted by Armored Dragon King Perugius, Dragon God Urupen, and North God Kalman I; defeated and sealed away, with his reincarnation prophesied in the late 5th century K-calendar.}
