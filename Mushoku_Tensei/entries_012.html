Claire Latreia

Keys: Claire Latreia, Claire

SHORT_DESC: {Matriarch of House Latreia, mother of Zenith Greyrat and Therese Latreia, and maternal grandmother of Rudeus, Norn, and Aisha [Birth: K368]; an imposing, severe, and iron-willed high noblewoman of the Holy Country of Milis; governed House Latreia with unyielding aristocratic discipline and staunch Milis orthodoxy; harbored deep, unresolved grief over Zenith running away to become an adventurer decades prior; clashed bitterly with Rudeus when he brought the memory-lost Zenith to Milishion, attempting to sequester Zenith into a cloistered sanitarium out of a twisted, protective desire to shield her daughter from adventurer danger; after Rudeus rescued Zenith and discovered the depths of Claire's secret maternal heartbreak, the two reconciled, with Claire acknowledging Rudeus's devotion and utilizing House Latreia's political influence to support the Orsted Corporation's anti-Hitogami campaigns in Milis | [Personality & Quirks] Stern, unbending, aristocratic, and fiercely prideful, mask-wearing to conceal deep emotional vulnerability; speaks in sharp, haughty noble cadences, demanding perfection and decorum from family members; deeply devout Milis believer who strictly adheres to church doctrines, yet willing to commit political treason if it means safeguarding her daughters; possesses profound, agonizing remorse over driving Zenith away in her youth, manifesting as overbearing, possessive protectiveness toward the handicapped Zenith; habit of gripping her silver cane and lifting her chin when challenged by lower nobles; secretly keeps a hidden portrait of young Zenith in her private study.}
