Keys: Alexander Rybak, Alec, North God Kalman III, Kalman III

SHORT_DESC: {The North God Kalman III, son of North God Kalman II (Sandor) and descendant of Immortal Demon Lord Atoferatofe [Birth: K405]; Rank 7 of the Seven Great World Powers, wielder of the mythical Dragon King Sword 'Kajakut' (Gravity Blade); an exceptionally handsome, half-demon youth possessing incredible physical speed, regenerative vigor, and mastery of all North God Style improvisation; obsessed from childhood with becoming a true, legendary 'Hero' of justice, viewing the world through romanticized knightly ideals and epic ballads; manipulated by Hitogami into believing that slaying Dragon God Orsted was his destiny to save humanity; served as the supreme vanguard champion of Hitogami's coalition in the Biheiril campaign, clashing against Rudeus in the Magic Armor MK-I, Eris, and Ruijerd; disarmed of Kajakut by Rudeus's tactical shotgun blast and crushed by Dragon God Orsted, who revealed that true heroism lies in protecting others rather than seeking glory; surrendered and joined the Orsted Corporation, being assigned to serve as the bodyguard and chief knight for Queen Ariel in Asura | [Personality & Quirks] Romantic, dramatic, boastful, and brimming with youthful theatricality; constantly delivers grandiloquent hero monologues, strikes heroic poses before drawing his blade, and names his attacks with flamboyant flair; has an innocent, childish worldview despite his terrifying martial strength, easily swayed by dramatic tales of heroism and righteous destiny; deeply loves his grandmother Atoferatofe and father Sandor, though he initially rebelled against his father's grounded, pragmatic advice; possesses an indomitable fighting spirit, refusing to stay down even when battered by god-class magic; after being humbled by Orsted, he matured into a genuinely noble, dedicated knight who uses his immense power for true selfless protection; loves listening to traveling bards, eating sweet pastries, and polishing his legendary blade.}
